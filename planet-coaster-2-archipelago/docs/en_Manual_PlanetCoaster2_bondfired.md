# Planet Coaster 2 (Manual)

## What is this?

A Manual Archipelago world for Planet Coaster 2's career campaign. There is no game
mod — you restrict what you build based on the items you receive, and you tick off
checks in the Manual client as you earn them.

## What is randomized?

Your ability to build things. Every coaster class, water slide type, flat ride,
scenery theme and park tool is an item somewhere in the multiworld. You start with
path tools, a Janitor, a Mechanic, one random coaster class and one random water
park piece, and you may only build with what you have been sent.

Career chapters are also gated. **Progressive Chapter Pass** unlocks Chapter 1
through Chapter 4 in order — even once the game itself has unlocked the next
chapter, you may not start it until the matching pass arrives.

## What are the locations?

The 72 career medals: bronze, silver, gold and platinum on each of the 18
scenarios across the Prologue and Chapters 1–4.

Turning on `franchise_milestones` adds 20 more checks that you can earn in
Franchise, Challenge or Sandbox parks — park ratings, guest counts, park value
and coaster excitement targets.

## What is the goal?

Set by the `goal` option:

- **career_complete** (default) — earn Gold on Coaster Chasm, the final scenario.
- **career_mastery** — earn Platinum on all 18 scenarios.

Whichever goal you do not pick stays in your world as an ordinary check.

## What counts as "having" an item?

- **Coaster classes** — you may build coasters of that track type only.
- **Water park items** — you may place that slide type, pool or river.
- **Flat rides** — you may place that ride.
- **Scenery themes** — you may use pieces from that theme's tab.
- **Park operations** — path tools, terrain sculpting, queue customisation, shops,
  marketing, lighting, blueprints.
- **Progressive Staff Hire** — hire one more staff type each copy, in the order
  Janitor, Mechanic, Vendor, Lifeguard. The first two are granted at the start, so
  the two you receive are the Vendor and the Lifeguard.

Anything a scenario hands you pre-built is fine to keep and operate. The
restriction is on what *you* place.

## Traps

With `include_traps` on, receiving a trap means sabotaging your own park: force a
ride breakdown, close for a downpour, cut the power. Honour system, like every
other rule here.

## Do I need to own any DLC?

No. Everything referenced is base-game content.
