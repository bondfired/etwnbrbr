# Planet Coaster 2 (Manual) Setup Guide

## Required Software

- Planet Coaster 2, any platform. No mods, no DLC.
- An [Archipelago](https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago/releases) install.
- The `manual_PlanetCoaster2_bondfired.apworld` file.

## Installation

1. Drop the `.apworld` into `custom_worlds/` in your Archipelago folder.
2. Launch the Archipelago Launcher once so it registers.

## Generating

1. Generate a template yaml from the launcher, or copy the one produced alongside
   the apworld.
2. Set your `name`, then pick `goal`, `franchise_milestones` and `include_traps`.
3. Put the yaml in `Players/` and run `ArchipelagoGenerate`.

Note that Manual worlds cannot be generated on the official website — generation
has to happen on a local install that has the apworld.

## Joining

Open **Manual Client** from the Archipelago Launcher, connect to the room, and
pick your slot. The client lists your locations grouped by chapter and shows the
items you have received.

## Playing

Start career mode. Build only with what the client says you have. When you earn a
medal, tick the matching location in the client.

The client cannot read the game, so nothing is enforced automatically — the
restrictions are yours to keep.

## Troubleshooting

**A scenario objective needs a ride I do not have.** That is expected; the check
stays locked until the item arrives. Work on another scenario in an unlocked
chapter, or bank progress and come back.

**I cannot reach the next chapter.** You need the next **Progressive Chapter
Pass**. Until it arrives, that chapter's medals are out of logic.
