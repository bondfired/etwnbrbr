How to Play

Guess today's AREDL demon in 5 guesses.

  • Each wrong guess reveals a new hint.
  • Hints go from vague to specific.
  • Use the autocomplete to find demons.
  • A new demon is chosen every day!

Hints revealed in order:

  • 🕐 Length (Short / Medium / Long / XL)
  • 🎮 Primary Skillset
  • 📊 List Position Tier
  • 📅 Publish & Rated Dates
  • 👤 Creator & Verifier
